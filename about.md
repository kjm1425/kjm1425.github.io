Hi! I'm Kylie — a dog-loving Seattleite, originally from Austin, Texas. 🐕🌲

By day, I'm a Product Manager at [Code.org](https://code.org), where I focus on
programming tools for the K-12 student experience, serving about 20 million
students annually. I partner with design, curriculum, and engineering teams to
build coding experiences that delight students and educators alike. Before
Code.org, I was a PM at Microsoft working on both Windows and Bing — at Bing, I
worked on search experiences including the results for queries about COVID-19
and the 2020 election.

Outside of work, I'm an individual with *many* hobbies — I could write a book
about them all (and in fact, [writing a novel](/projects/the-novel/) is one of
them). A few highlights:

**Music is my oldest hobby** and a reliable constant in my life. I love listening
to it, singing along, and making it myself. My first instrument was the recorder
in elementary school, then the violin, and in middle school I found my love for
the flute. Ever since, I've been a flautist performing in symphonic wind
ensembles, orchestras, and smaller groups including an all-flute choir. Most
recently, I've been learning and performing in a
[jazz tango ensemble](/projects/jazz-tango/) taught by an amazing bandoneon
instructor — we perform every few months at The Royal Room, a local jazz club.
I've dabbled in arranging pieces, and hope to one day try composing.

**Disc golf is my newest hobby.** I'd noticed the baskets around local parks for
years, but only recently tried it — and now it's become a weekly ritual in my
household. My husband, Adrian, has fallen in love with the sport too, and our two
dogs often join us on the course. Recent personal record:
[three (3!!!) pars over 18 holes](/projects/disc-golf/).

I also paint, sew, doodle dress designs, and generally
[collect ideas](/projects/idea-garden/) faster than I can act on them.

I've spent most of my career making search better, in three quite different
worlds.

At Microsoft, I was a PM on Bing, working on search experiences including the
results people saw for queries about COVID-19 and the 2020 election —
high-stakes moments where getting information right really mattered.

At DoorDash, I focused on search user experiences: making sure you can find
anything you need locally, and enabling discovery of new merchant offerings you
didn't know to look for.

Today I'm a Senior Product Manager at Yahoo, driving search growth — from
integrations across first-party experiences to targeted improvements to the
search results page itself.

Different domains, same core puzzle: someone types a few words into a box and
trusts you to figure out what they actually meant. I haven't gotten tired of
that puzzle yet.

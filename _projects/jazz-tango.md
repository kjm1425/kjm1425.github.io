Music is my oldest hobby and a reliable constant in my life. It started with the
recorder in elementary school, moved to the violin soon after, and then in middle
school I found my love for the flute. Ever since, I've been a flautist —
performing in symphonic wind ensembles, orchestras, and smaller groups, including
an all-flute choir.

My current musical adventure is a jazz tango ensemble, taught by an amazing
bandoneon instructor. We learn new music together and perform every few months at
[The Royal Room](https://theroyalroomseattle.com), a jazz club in Seattle's
Columbia City neighborhood. Tango is a whole different musical language from the
classical repertoire I grew up on, and I love it.

I've also dabbled in arranging pieces, and one day I hope to try composing new
music of my own. If you're in Seattle, come catch a show!

From 2021 to 2022, I was a Product Manager at [Code.org](https://code.org),
focused on the programming tools behind the K-12 student experience — App Lab,
Sprite Lab, and Game Lab, the editors and creative environments that roughly
20 million students a year used to write their first lines of code.

I partnered with design, curriculum, and engineering teams to create
experiences that delight both students and educators. It's a special kind of
product problem: your users range from kindergartners to high school seniors to
the teachers guiding them, and the goal isn't engagement for its own sake —
it's genuine learning and creative confidence.

Fittingly, the seed for this work was planted early: at a Three Day Startup
hackathon back in 2014, the idea I pitched was combining storytelling and
coding to make computer science more accessible for kids.
[I wrote about that experience here](/3DS/).

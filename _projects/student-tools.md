I'm a Product Manager at [Code.org](https://code.org), where I focus on the
programming tools behind the K-12 student experience — the editors, environments,
and creative spaces that roughly 20 million students use each year to write their
first lines of code.

I partner with design, curriculum, and engineering teams to create experiences
that delight both students and educators. It's a special kind of product problem:
your users range from kindergartners to high school seniors to the teachers
guiding them, and the goal isn't engagement for its own sake — it's genuine
learning and creative confidence.

Before Code.org, I was a Program Manager at Microsoft, working on both Windows
and Bing. At Bing I worked on search experiences, including the results people
saw for queries about COVID-19 and the 2020 election — high-stakes moments where
getting information right really mattered.

Fittingly, the seed for all of this was planted early: at a Three Day Startup
hackathon back in 2014, the idea I pitched was combining storytelling and coding
to make computer science more accessible for kids.
[I wrote about that experience here](/3DS/).

I always find myself looking for creative projects. Painting is the steady one —
but the full list is longer and weirder.

I've taken a costume technology course and learned to sew; I still doodle dress
designs in the margins of my notebooks. I've arranged music. I've prototyped
board game ideas. The medium changes, but the itch is the same: take a fuzzy idea
and make it into a real thing you can hold, hear, or wear.

This page is a home for those one-off explorations as they happen — a place to
document them more meaningfully than leaving them as disparate thoughts in my
head.

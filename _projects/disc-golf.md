I'd seen the baskets around Seattle parks for years, but I only threw my first
disc recently — and now disc golf has fully taken over my household, replacing my
usual go-tos of swimming and dancing for physical activity.

My husband, Adrian, fell in love with the sport just as fast. We play at least
once a week at a local course, sometimes with our two dogs along for the round.
We're building a small collection of discs, and we're contemplating a private
lesson from a pro.

Current personal record: **three (3!!!) pars** over an 18-hole course. It has
been genuinely rewarding to watch our progress — I wouldn't be surprised if we're
entering our first amateur tournament before long. Updates as they land
(hopefully near the basket).

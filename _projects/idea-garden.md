As a product manager, I get ideas *all the time* — from daydreaming about owning
my own spa to inventing a better sand timer for board game night. For years those
thoughts lived as disparate scraps in my head and my notebooks.

So I started gardening them. I keep a running map of the things I work on and
think about, organized so I can see how they connect and watch new clusters
sprout over time. The [home page](/) shows a rotating sample from the garden —
a handful of seeds at a time, replanted on every visit.

Why a sample and not the whole thing? Part of the fun of a garden is that you
can't see all of it from the gate. The full tangle is bigger, messier, and
always changing — and some seedlings need time in the greenhouse before they're
ready for visitors.

If you're curious what the whole map looks like from above,
[the unabridged version lives on Flourish ↗](https://public.flourish.studio/visualisation/19218225/).

Like any garden, it's never finished — check back and it will have grown.
